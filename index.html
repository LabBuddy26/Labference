Abstract

LabBench is a chemistry laboratory manipulation dataset and benchmark for embodied agents. It comprises 24 ordinary long-horizon and reasoning-in-action tasks and 4 selected special long-horizon workflows, supported by a catalogue of real simulator assets and synchronized RGB, robot-state, joint-action, end-effector-action and gripper recordings. Successful collection demonstrations are currently presented for weighing and device operation; centrifuge loading and rack recall remain pending public implementation.

Task completion is evaluated through explicit physical predicates derived from independent simulator state rather than controller phase completion alone. The special workflows and ordinary benchmark are reported in separate evaluation groups, preserving their task-specific metrics and distinguishing collection demonstrations from model evaluation results.
